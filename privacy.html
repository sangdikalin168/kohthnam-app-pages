Koh Thnam App – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 3 September 2026

This policy explains what the Koh Thnam mobile application (“the App”), operated by Koh Thnam Pharmacy (“we”, “us”), does with your information. We designed the App to keep as much as possible on your device.

1. Information stored only on your device

Your sign-in name and email, shopping cart, wishlist, saved addresses and order history are stored locally on your phone. They are not uploaded to our servers. Deleting the App removes them.

2. Information shared to process a payment

When you pay for an order with KHQR, the App sends the following to ABA Bank’s PayWay payment gateway so that a payment request can be created and confirmed:

DataPurpose
Your first and last nameIdentify the payer on the payment request
Your phone numberPayment request and order contact
Order lines and total amountCreate the KHQR payment and confirm it

ABA Bank processes this data under its own privacy policy (ababank.com). We do not receive or store your bank account or card details; the payment is completed inside your banking app.

3. What we do not do

  • We do not use advertising or analytics SDKs.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • We do not sell personal information.
  • We do not collect your location.

4. QR code rendering

The App normally displays the KHQR code returned by PayWay. If that image is unavailable, the App may generate the QR image through a third-party QR rendering service; the QR payload contains the merchant identifier and order amount only.

5. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

6. Your choices

You can sign out at any time from the Account tab, which clears your local sign-in details. To ask about data sent to PayWay for a specific order, contact us with the order number.

7. Changes

We will post any changes to this page and update the effective date above.
