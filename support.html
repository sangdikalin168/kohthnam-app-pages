Koh Thnam is the ordering app of Koh Thnam Pharmacy in Cambodia. Browse medicines, balms, first-aid and personal-care products, pay with KHQR, and have your order delivered.

How it works

Placing an order

  1. Browse the Shop tab or search for a product, then add it to your cart.
  2. Open the Cart tab and tap Checkout. Enter your name, phone number and delivery address.
  3. Choose ABA KHQR or Acleda KHQR and tap Place Order.
  4. Scan the KHQR code with any Cambodian banking app, or tap Open ABA Mobile App. The order is confirmed automatically once the payment clears.

Questions

FAQ

The KHQR code expired before I paid.

Each code is valid for 5 minutes. Go back to your cart and place the order again; nothing is charged for an expired code.

I paid but the app still says “Waiting for payment”.

Wait a few seconds; the app checks the payment automatically. If it doesn’t update, note the order number under Account → Orders and contact us with it.

Do I need an account?

Sign in with any email and password to keep your cart and orders on your phone. No account data is stored on our servers.

Do you sell prescription medicines?

Prescription medicines are dispensed in accordance with Cambodian regulations. Our pharmacists may contact you before delivery.

How do I delete my account and data?

Open the Account tab, scroll down and tap Delete Account, then confirm. This permanently removes your account, saved address, cart, wishlist and order history. Everything lives only on your phone, so nothing remains on our servers.
